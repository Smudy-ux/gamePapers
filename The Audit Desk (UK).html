The Audit Desk

Papers come in. Cross-check them against the rulebook, spot the trap, and stamp your decision before the case timer runs out.

  • Each case has its own countdown. Run out of time and it counts as a strike.
  • Every 3 correct stamps in a row, the countdown gets 2 seconds shorter.
  • Stuck? Put one case on the hold tray and come back to it.
  • Flagging the lines that expose a problem earns bonus points. Every shift is freshly generated, so the answers change each time.

Rules and figures follow England in the 2026/27 tax year. Fictional cases, for learning. This is not financial advice.
